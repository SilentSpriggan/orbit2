Reference data: BodyParts3D 4.0, adult male anatomy. The user supplied the atlas manifest and 15 packed binary geometry chunks. The manifest reports geometry simplification with meshoptimizer at 0.2% maximum relative error, axis/unit conversion, and quantized normals. ORBIT uses these supplied geometry files without modifying their vertex positions, and adds display layers, selection colors, and record annotations.

The reference model does not depict an individual crew member, a medical scan, or changes caused by a recorded condition. Linked regions are visual record annotations only.
